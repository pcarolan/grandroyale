Pepperoni Pie Test batch · v1

Grand Royale Pizza, Petoskey. One 12-inch pie on a 260 g Roberta's ball. Deck oven, not wood-fired. Five things on the dough.

1. Dough (Roberta's, two balls)

IngredientGrams
00 flour153
All-purpose flour153
Fine sea salt8
Active dry yeast2
Extra-virgin olive oil4
Lukewarm water200
  1. Flours + salt in a bowl. Stir water, yeast, oil together; pour in.
  2. Knead a few minutes. Rest 15 min. Knead 3 min more.
  3. Divide in two (260 g each), ball, damp cloth.
  4. Rise 3-4 h at room temp, or fridge 8-24 h (up to several days), then 45 min out before shaping.
  5. Stretch by hand to 12 in, 1-in rim. Never a rolling pin.

65% hydration, half 00 / half AP.

2. Sauce (raw, Roberta's way)

  • Whole peeled tomatoes, drained, juice out. Bianco DiNapoli first pick; Stanislaus Alta Cucina for value. Not crushed, not purée.
  • Blitz nearly smooth + a splash of olive oil + fine salt (about 1% of drained weight). Taste. Do not cook.
  • 85 g per pie, spread to a 1-in rim.

3. Cheese

  • Low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, grated from the block. Grande WM loaf; Galbani WM as the stand-in. Never pre-shredded, never part-skim, no fresh mozz on this pie.
  • 140 g per pie, applied sparingly so the top goes mottled red / white / brown.

4. Pepperoni

  • Cup-and-char, natural or collagen casing: Ezzo GiAntonio (Supreme if you want it hotter and blacker). Sticks: Vermont Smoke & Cure or Boar's Head, hand-sliced 2.5-3 mm.
  • 28 slices, 38 mm, about 42 g, laid on top of the cheese so the lip cooks first and cups. About 60% coverage raw; they shrink.
