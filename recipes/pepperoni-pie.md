# Grand Royale pepperoni pie: the toppings spec

**TL;DR.** On one 260 g Roberta's dough ball stretched to 12 inches: **85 g raw tomato sauce** (drained whole peeled tomatoes blitzed with a splash of olive oil and salt, Roberta's method), **140 g low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella** grated from the block, **28 slices (~42 g) of collagen/natural-casing cup-and-char pepperoni** (Ezzo GiAntonio; Ezzo sliced runs 38 mm, 18-20 per oz per the Supreme listing) laid **on top of** the cheese, bake on the deck at 550-600°F for about 6-8 minutes, then finish with **a pinch of dried oregano and ~3 g grated pecorino romano**. Hot honey is an add-on, not the default. Five ingredients on the dough. Built for Grand Royale Pizza (Petoskey, MI) on the Roberta's dough in this folder (robertas-pizza-dough.pdf).

## 1. Tomato

- **Method: raw, Roberta's style.** Roberta's own sauce is a 28 oz can of San Marzano whole peeled tomatoes, drained (juice discarded), pureed nearly smooth, plus "a splash" of good olive oil and a pinch of fine sea salt, tasted and adjusted. No cooking. ([Edible Brooklyn, Roberta's cookbook excerpt](https://ediblebrooklyn.com/2013/recipe-robertas-margherita-pizza-and-cookbook-giveaway/))
- **Why raw:** Neapolitan pies "generally use a simple, uncooked sauce of fresh or canned tomatoes and salt"; New York slice pies use a heavily seasoned cooked sauce (Kenji's: butter, oil, garlic, oregano, onion, basil, sugar, chili, 1 hour simmer). ([Serious Eats NY sauce](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza-sauce)) Raw fits a 50/50 00 dough and "simple"; cooked is the fallback if Todd wants a sweeter NY slice.
- **Which can: whole peeled, not crushed.** Kenji: crushed varies wildly by brand, purée and paste taste too cooked, diced contains calcium chloride and won't break down; buy whole peeled in juice and crush your own. ([Serious Eats NY sauce](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza-sauce))
  - **First pick: Bianco DiNapoli Whole Peeled**, organic, California-grown, #10 can (102 oz), packed with basil and salt (so taste before salting). ([Bianco DiNapoli products](https://www.biancodinapoli.com/products))
  - **Value pick: Stanislaus Alta Cucina** whole plum tomatoes, "naturale" light juice with fresh basil, 6.43 lb foodservice can. ([igourmet](https://igourmet.com/products/whole-peeled-tomatoes-by-stanislaus)) Stanislaus **7/11** is ground *unpeeled* tomatoes meant to be blended into a thicker pizza sauce base, not used alone. ([Stanislaus](https://stanislaus.com/product/7-11-ground-unpeeled-tomatoes/))
  - **San Marzano DOP:** what Roberta's specifies ([Edible Brooklyn](https://ediblebrooklyn.com/2013/recipe-robertas-margherita-pizza-and-cookbook-giveaway/)); more expensive and adds nothing a pepperoni pie can taste through. Skip unless a blind test says otherwise.
- **How much:** Roberta's uses **43 g (3 tbsp)** on a 12-inch Margherita ([Edible Brooklyn](https://ediblebrooklyn.com/2013/recipe-robertas-margherita-pizza-and-cookbook-giveaway/)); the Burke operator portion guide uses **4 oz (113 g)** on a 12-inch ([Burke](https://www.burkecorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Portion-Guide.pdf)). Spec **85 g**, in between: the pie carries more cheese and pepperoni fat than a Margherita, but the thin 260 g ball can't take a full NY pour.
- **Salt:** start around 1% of drained tomato weight and adjust by taste (house starting point, not sourced).

## 2. Cheese

- **Low-moisture, whole-milk mozzarella, grated from the block.** Kenji: NY pies use dry mozzarella; it "must be full-fat" (part-skim doesn't stretch right) and "do not buy pre-shredded" (anti-caking starch stops a proper melt). ([Serious Eats NY pizza](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza)) The Takeout's pizzaiolo sources agree: shred your own, cellulose makes it grainy. ([The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/the-best-mozzarella-according-to-pizza-pros-1849625114/)) (ATK dissents mildly: pre-shred melts smoother, but it still prefers block. [ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2786-all-about-mozzarella))
- **Brand:**
  - **Grande Whole Milk** is the pizzeria benchmark: "slightly softer and creamier," higher butterfat; loaf 8 x 7 lb (#00106), also shredded/diced. ([Grande](https://grandecheese.com/cheeses/mozzarella/whole-milk-mozzarella/))
  - **Galbani Whole Milk** and **Polly-O Whole Milk** won Serious Eats' 36-cheese blind test ([Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/low-moisture-mozzarella-taste-test-12137202)) and ATK's block test ([ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/taste_tests/2786-all-about-mozzarella)). "The dominant brands in most pizzerias are Galbani and Grande" (Derrick Tung, Paulie Gee's Logan Square). ([The Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/the-best-mozzarella-according-to-pizza-pros-1849625114/))
  - Pick: **Grande WM loaf** if a distributor carries it; Galbani WM as the stand-in.
- **No fresh mozzarella.** Roberta's Margherita uses 80 g house-made fresh mozz ([Edible Brooklyn](https://ediblebrooklyn.com/2013/recipe-robertas-margherita-pizza-and-cookbook-giveaway/)); on a pepperoni pie its water pools with the grease. Low-moisture only.
- **Hard cheese: a little pecorino romano, as a finish.** Prince Street's Spicy Spring uses pecorino romano; Kenji: real imported block, grated in-house. ([Serious Eats Spicy Spring](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe))
- **How much:** Burke guide **6 oz (170 g)** per 12-inch ([Burke](https://www.burkecorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Portion-Guide.pdf)); Kenji's NY recipe ~151 g per 12-14-inch pie (1 lb over 3) and says apply it "sparingly" so the top is mottled red, white and brown ([Serious Eats NY pizza](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza)). Spec **140 g**.

## 3. Pepperoni

- **Cup-and-char, not flat.** Ezzo, to Kenji: Classic pepperoni in fibrous casing (stripped before slicing) lays flat; GiAntonio in collagen casing, sliced in its casing, shrinks and cups. Natural or collagen casing changes how the meat packs (a U-shaped density pattern), so it curls even with the casing peeled. The cup's lip crisps and holds rendered fat. ([Serious Eats pepperoni curl](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-pizza-lab-why-does-pepperoni-curl))
- **Thickness:** in Kenji's hand-sliced tests curl starts around 2.5 mm; best between 2.5 and 5.6 mm; thinnest slices (1.3 mm) got swallowed by cheese. ([Serious Eats pepperoni curl](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-pizza-lab-why-does-pepperoni-curl)) His Spicy Spring recipe slices at 1/8 inch (~3 mm). ([Serious Eats Spicy Spring](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe)) Commercial cup-and-char runs thinner and still cups: Ezzo Supreme is 38 mm at 18-20 slices per oz ([BoxNCase](https://www.boxncase.com/products/ezzo-cup-char-pepperoni-supreme-25lb)), which works out to roughly 1.3-1.5 mm (our arithmetic). So the 1.5-2 mm in the brief is right for pre-sliced; hand-sliced sticks should go to 2.5-3 mm.
- **Brands:**
  - **Ezzo GiAntonio (pick).** Pork picnic and beef, dried 14 days, collagen casing, light brown edges; **Supreme** is hotter and "burns charcoal black around the edges." 2/5 lb sliced. ([Ezzo spec sheet via Accardi](https://www.accardifoods.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Ezzo-Pepperoni.pdf)) Wholesale only; 25 lb case ~$308 at one distributor. ([Eccolo](https://eccolofoods.com/products/ezzo-pepperoni-giantonio-sliced-pepperoni-case-25lb)) Kenji calls Ezzo "one of the finest pepperoni makers." ([Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-pizza-lab-why-does-pepperoni-curl))
  - **Vermont Smoke & Cure:** Kenji's favorite for the home Spicy Spring ([Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe)); smoked, uncured, no-antibiotics pork ([VS&C](https://www.vermontsmokeandcure.com/products/pepperoni-summer-sausage/uncured-smoked-pepperoni/)); 6 oz sticks, so hand-slicing.
  - **Boar's Head Traditional:** natural edible casing, air-dried ([Boar's Head](https://boarshead.com/products/detail/16078-pepperoni-natural-casing-6-5-oz-net-wt)); Kenji's test stick. Retail sticks, fine for R&D.
  - **Margherita Cup n Char:** collagen casing, 2/12.5 lb sliced ([Smithfield Culinary](https://smithfieldculinary.com/margherita/our-products-api/)).
  - **Hormel Cup N' Crisp:** collagen casing, 5 oz retail bag ([Hormel](https://www.hormel.com/brands/hormel-pepperoni/product/cup-and-crisp-pepperoni/)).
  - One blind cup-and-taste ranking (no Ezzo in it): Hormel 9.15, Margherita 8.85, Battistoni Cup & Char 8.8, Vermont Smoke & Cure 7.85, Boar's Head 7.55. ([NEPA Pizza Review](https://nepapizzareview.com/2024/03/pepperoni-power-rankings-testing-the-best-cup-n-char.html))
- **Who uses what:** Prince Street's Spicy Spring uses spicy natural-casing pepperoni covering the pie ([Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe)); Mama's Too hand-slices theirs ([Edible Manhattan](https://ediblemanhattan.com/eat/mamas-too-the-neighborhood-slice-shop-with-innovative-energy/)). Neither names a brand publicly. Roberta's (at least on an archived menu) has no pepperoni pie: its cured-meat pies use soppressata, and its Famous Original is tomato, mozzarella, caciocavallo, oregano, chili ([SinglePlatform menu](http://places.singleplatform.com/robertas-pizza/menu)); current menu: verify.
- **On top of the cheese.** The exposed lip cooks faster than the base insulated by cheese and crust, which drives the cup. ([Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-pizza-lab-why-does-pepperoni-curl))
- **How many:** Burke guide 20 slices / 1.25 oz per 12-inch ([Burke](https://www.burkecorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Portion-Guide.pdf)); Kenji aims for 70-80% coverage because slices shrink ([Serious Eats Spicy Spring](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe)). Spec **28 slices of 38 mm (~42 g)**, about 60% raw coverage inside a 1-inch rim: more than a chain pie, less than a Spicy Spring.

## 4. Finish

- **Keep:** pinch of **dried oregano** (oregano keeps its flavor dried; Kenji found barely any difference vs fresh) ([Serious Eats NY sauce](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza-sauce)); **~3 g grated pecorino romano** ([Serious Eats Spicy Spring](https://www.seriouseats.com/spicy-spring-sicilian-pizza-recipe)).
- **Optional:** a thin thread of olive oil (Roberta's drizzles oil on its Margherita before the cheese ([Edible Brooklyn](https://ediblebrooklyn.com/2013/recipe-robertas-margherita-pizza-and-cookbook-giveaway/)); with cup-and-char fat, oil on top is redundant).
- **Add-on, not default: Mike's Hot Honey.** Born on Paulie Gee's pepperoni "Hellboy" ([Slate](https://slate.com/life/2025/11/mikes-hot-honey-near-me-ingredients-pizza-recipes.html)), still a bestseller there ([Mike's](https://mikeshothoney.com/blogs/news/how-mikes-hot-honey-sparked-the-sweet-heat-trend-starting-with-paulie-gees-brooklyn-pizzeria)). Sell it as a named variant or upcharge.
- **Clutter (judgment):** fresh basil (it belongs on the Margherita, and wilts black on a hot deck), garlic, chili flake by default, ranch, a second cheese blend.

## 5. Assembly and quantities (one 12-inch pie)

| Step | Item | Amount |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | Roberta's dough ball, stretched to 12 in | 260 g (520 g batch / 2) (robertas-pizza-dough.pdf) |
| 2 | Raw sauce, spread to a 1 in rim | 85 g |
| 3 | Low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, grated | 140 g |
| 4 | Cup-and-char pepperoni, 38 mm, on top | 28 slices, ~42 g |
| 5 | Bake (gas deck 550-600°F; check at 5 min) | ~6-8 min |
| 6 | Dried oregano + grated pecorino romano | pinch + ~3 g |
| opt | Olive oil thread / hot honey (variant) | ~2 g / ~10 g |

- **Bake guidance:** deck ovens run 300-700°F on gas or electric and generally cook pizzas in about five minutes; electric decks add a top element and can run decks at different temperatures. ([FES Magazine](https://fesmag.com/products/guide/cooking-equipment/ovens/15503-the-quarterly-product-knowledge-guide-deck-ovens)) Modern NY gas ovens run 500-550°F ([Serious Eats NY pizza](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza)). Bake until the pepperoni cups and the cheese is mottled with brown spots. Bake time and step 6 amounts are starting points to dial in on the real oven.
