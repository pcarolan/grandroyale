# Recipes

Working recipes for Grand Royale Pizza.

- `robertas-pizza-dough.pdf` — Roberta's (Bushwick, Brooklyn) pizza dough, as published in the New York Times and mirrored by Bewitching Kitchen (source: https://bewitchingkitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/robertapizzadough.pdf). Added 2026-09-30.
- Video: [ROBERTA'S PIZZA Recipe | The most popular pizza recipe online | New York Times Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UaIXCEy_VtY&t=5s) by Parks and Pizza (YouTube). Walkthrough of the same NYT Roberta's dough as the PDF above. Added 2026-10-01.
- `pepperoni-pie.md` — Pepperoni pie toppings spec for the Roberta's dough: raw whole-peeled tomato sauce (85 g), low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella (140 g), 28 slices cup-and-char pepperoni (Ezzo GiAntonio), oregano + pecorino finish; per-12-inch quantities, deck bake, Petoskey sourcing. Added 2026-10-01.

- `pepperoni-pie-card.pdf` / `.html` — one-page kitchen card for the pepperoni pie (typewriter, black + red), condensed from `pepperoni-pie.md`. 2026-10-01.
